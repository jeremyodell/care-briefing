Approach

Adapt, don't build from scratch key decision

Strip the towncrier civic-advisor harness to its two highest-value roles: a Researcher (cite-or-decline, adapted from advisor.md) and a Publisher (self-contained HTML export, near-verbatim from publisher.md). Pitch line: a production-shaped harness, retargeted to a new domain in under two hours.

Pre-loaded corpus, not live web search

One markdown file per condition (frontmatter + 3 cited sections: Standard of Care, Emerging Treatments, Key Companies & Institutions). No live-internet dependency during the interview. Claude synthesizes the on-screen briefing from only that file's content — an out-of-corpus question gets an explicit decline, not a fabricated answer.
