# Type 2 Diabetes

## Standard of Care

The American Diabetes Association published its **Standards of Care in Diabetes—2026** on December 8, 2025, as a supplement to *Diabetes Care* [ADA Standards of Care 2026 Press Release](https://diabetes.org/newsroom/press-releases/american-diabetes-association-releases-standards-care-diabetes-2026). Intensive lifestyle intervention plus metformin remains first-line pharmacotherapy for most adults with type 2 diabetes, but the 2026 edition reinforces early, comorbidity-driven addition of agents with proven cardiovascular, renal, and weight benefit — an SGLT2 inhibitor or a GLP-1 receptor agonist — in addition to or independent of metformin, especially for patients with established cardiovascular disease, heart failure, chronic kidney disease, or obesity [ADA Ch. 9: Pharmacologic Approaches to Glycemic Treatment](https://diabetesjournals.org/care/article/49/Supplement_1/S183/163934/9-Pharmacologic-Approaches-to-Glycemic-Treatment). Therapy selection is person-centered, weighing hypoglycemia risk, treatment burden, and patient preference rather than following a single fixed sequence.

On monitoring, the general HbA1c target remains <7% for most adults, with a corresponding time-in-range (TIR) goal of over 70%, time below 70 mg/dL kept under 4%, and time below 54 mg/dL kept under 1% [ADA Ch. 6: Glycemic Goals, Hypoglycemia, and Hyperglycemic Crises](https://diabetesjournals.org/care/article/49/Supplement_1/S132/163927/6-Glycemic-Goals-Hypoglycemia-and-Hyperglycemic). A notable 2026 shift: continuous glucose monitoring (CGM) is now recommended at diabetes onset and at any point thereafter for anyone who could benefit — insulin users, those at hypoglycemia risk, and increasingly those on non-insulin regimens — broadening CGM eligibility well beyond the historical insulin-only criteria [ADA Standards of Care 2026 Press Release](https://diabetes.org/newsroom/press-releases/american-diabetes-association-releases-standards-care-diabetes-2026).

## Emerging Treatments in Development

**Elecoglipron** (AstraZeneca), an oral small-molecule GLP-1 receptor agonist, advanced to a Phase III program (ELUMINATE) in 2026 after Phase IIb data showed a 1.9% mean HbA1c reduction and 7.7% body-weight reduction at 26 weeks in adults with type 2 diabetes; ELUMINATE will test elecoglipron as monotherapy and combined with dapagliflozin, alongside dedicated cardiovascular/kidney outcome trials [HCPLive: Elecoglipron Advances to Phase 3](https://www.hcplive.com/view/elecoglipron-phase-3-shows-phase-2-weight-loss-glycemic-data).

**Survodutide**, a glucagon/GLP-1 receptor dual agonist co-developed by Boehringer Ingelheim and Zealand Pharma, is in the Phase III SYNCHRONIZE-2 trial enrolling adults with obesity and type 2 diabetes (A1C 6.5%–<10%) [PubMed: SYNCHRONIZE-2 baseline characteristics](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41216778/); [Boehringer Ingelheim: Survodutide Phase III](https://www.boehringer-ingelheim.com/human-health/metabolic-diseases/survodutide-phase-iii-study-weight-loss).

**CagriSema** (cagrilintide + semaglutide, Novo Nordisk) completed a Phase III head-to-head trial against tirzepatide 15 mg in adults with type 2 diabetes inadequately controlled on metformin ± an SGLT2 inhibitor [ClinicalTrials.gov NCT06221969](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06221969).

**Retatrutide**, Eli Lilly's GLP-1/GIP/glucagon triple agonist, is in the Phase III TRANSCEND-T2D program and has shown significant HbA1c reductions versus placebo [PharmExec: Orforglipron, Retatrutide Named Defining GLP-1 Drugs](https://www.pharmexec.com/view/eli-lilly-orforglipron-retatrutide-glp-clarivate). Lilly's oral GLP-1 **orforglipron** (brand name Foundayo) was FDA-approved for chronic weight management in adults with obesity/overweight in April 2026; Lilly has stated it will file a separate type 2 diabetes application based on the Phase III ACHIEVE program [HCPLive: Orforglipron Receives FDA Approval](https://www.hcplive.com/view/diabetes-dialogue-orforglipron-receives-fda-approval-for-chronic-weight-management).

Two agents new to the standard of care from recent FDA approvals: **bexagliflozin** (Brenzavvy, TheracosBio), an SGLT2 inhibitor approved January 20, 2023 [HCPLive: Bexagliflozin Receives FDA Approval](https://www.hcplive.com/view/bexagliflozin-brenzavvy-receives-fda-approval-for-type-2-diabetes-in-adults); and **insulin icodec** (Awiqli, Novo Nordisk), the first once-weekly basal insulin approved for type 2 diabetes, cleared March 26, 2026 based on the Phase III ONWARDS program (2,680 adults) [Pharmacy Times: FDA Approves Insulin Icodec-abae](https://www.pharmacytimes.com/view/fda-approves-insulin-icodec-abae-first-once-weekly-basal-insulin-for-adults-with-t2d).

## Key Companies & Institutions

- **Eli Lilly** — developer of tirzepatide (Mounjaro/Zepbound), orforglipron (Foundayo, ACHIEVE program for a type 2 diabetes indication), and retatrutide (TRANSCEND-T2D) [PharmExec](https://www.pharmexec.com/view/eli-lilly-orforglipron-retatrutide-glp-clarivate).
- **Novo Nordisk** — sponsor of the ONWARDS trials supporting Awiqli (insulin icodec) and the Phase III CagriSema-vs-tirzepatide trial [Pharmacy Times](https://www.pharmacytimes.com/view/fda-approves-insulin-icodec-abae-first-once-weekly-basal-insulin-for-adults-with-t2d); [ClinicalTrials.gov NCT06221969](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06221969).
- **AstraZeneca** — advancing elecoglipron through the Phase III ELUMINATE program [HCPLive](https://www.hcplive.com/view/elecoglipron-phase-3-shows-phase-2-weight-loss-glycemic-data).
- **Boehringer Ingelheim** and **Zealand Pharma** — co-developing survodutide, in Phase III SYNCHRONIZE-2 for type 2 diabetes with obesity [Boehringer Ingelheim](https://www.boehringer-ingelheim.com/human-health/metabolic-diseases/survodutide-phase-iii-study-weight-loss).
- **TheracosBio** — developer of bexagliflozin (Brenzavvy), FDA-approved January 2023 [HCPLive](https://www.hcplive.com/view/bexagliflozin-brenzavvy-receives-fda-approval-for-type-2-diabetes-in-adults).
- **American Diabetes Association** — publisher of the annual Standards of Care in Diabetes guideline referenced throughout this briefing [ADA Standards of Care 2026](https://diabetes.org/newsroom/press-releases/american-diabetes-association-releases-standards-care-diabetes-2026).
